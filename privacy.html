Privacy at Pip Maths

Last updated: 25 November 2026

The short version. Pip Maths needs a family account, made by a parent on the Uplore home page. We store the parent's email and the children's first names or nicknames, ages and maths progress, so it works on every device. We never sell data, never show ads, never ask children for their email, and a parent can delete everything at any time.

Who we are

Pip Maths is made by Uplore. For any privacy question or request, contact us by email (contact address to be confirmed before launch).

Why an account is needed

A parent signs in before any lessons open. That keeps every child's progress backed up and the same on each device, and it means children never type an email or a password. A copy of the learners is also kept in this browser so lessons work smoothly; signing out removes it from the device.

What a family account stores

WhatWhy
Parent's email and password (the password is stored scrambled; we cannot read it)To sign in, and to send sign-up and password-reset emails
Each child's first name or nickname, age, colour and chosen age groupTo show the right lessons and the child's own profile
Progress: answers right or wrong per lesson, best scores, stickers, coins, practice timesSo progress and the dashboard are the same on every device
Settings: country, sound and voiceSo numbers, money and units match your country

We do not collect: children's emails, surnames, photos, voice recordings, location, contacts or advertising IDs. Spoken prompts are made by your device's own voice; nothing is recorded.

Children

Family accounts are only for parents and guardians, who confirm this when signing up. Children use the app as learners under the parent's account and are never asked for personal contact details.

Who can see the data

  • You: only your own family's learners, on devices where you sign in.
  • Uplore: only totals across all families (such as how many families use the app and which lessons are most practised), to improve the lessons. Our admin view shows no names.
  • Our service provider: data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in), which processes it for us under its data processing terms.

We never sell data or share it for advertising.

Your choices

  • See or change it: everything is shown in the app (Grown-ups, Dashboard). You can edit names and ages there.
  • Delete it: Grown-ups → Family account → Delete account removes the account and every child's saved progress from our systems straight away. They are removed from this device too.
  • Stop syncing: Sign out at any time. The app keeps working on the device.

How long we keep it

Until you delete the account. If an account is not used for 3 years, we may delete it after emailing the parent.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules make sure each family can only reach its own learners.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell parents in the app before the change applies.
